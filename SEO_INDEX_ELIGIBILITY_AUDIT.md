# SEO Index Eligibility Audit

## 2026-10-05 revision: generate only what should be indexed

Supersedes the tables below. Driven by the GSC export of 2026-10-05: State × bird
and city pages were the only page type ranking (146 URLs, 753 impressions,
weighted position 18.2, 4 of 5 clicks) while most of them were `noindex`, and
5,000+ `noindex, follow` pages were absorbing crawl.

Rule: a page is either `index, follow` and in the sitemap, or it is not generated
(404) and nothing links to it. `lib/indexing.ts` is the single source of truth
for page metadata, `generateStaticParams`, internal links and `app/sitemap.xml`.

| Route class | Published (index) | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| State × bird | ~1,230 | GSC whitelist (`data/gsc-location-pages.ts`) ∪ eBird occurrence data in the state with abundance ≥ uncommon |
| City | 4 | GSC whitelist only (Stamford, Springfield IL, St. George, Biloxi) |
| Bird profiles | ~115 | U.S. backyard species (100-bird whitelist + reviewed + yard regulars) with editorial, reviewed data, or eBird evidence; the rest of the 1,000-species AOS catalog is not generated |
| Plant / feeder / seasonal state previews | 0 | Not generated until they have their own data |
| `/birds/seasonal` | — | 308 → `/seasonal-birds` |

eBird occurrence data now covers 19 states (added nebraska, illinois, virginia,
south-dakota, hawaii, missouri, georgia, wyoming, kansas, iowa by GSC impression
priority). Rare/absent "Are there X in Y?" pages stay indexed when whitelisted
and open with a direct yes/no answer, the nearest states with a regular
population, and local look-alikes.

Sitemap `<lastmod>` is the newest git commit date of the files each URL is
rendered from (`data/lastmod.json`, regenerated by `npm run build` via
`scripts/generate-lastmod.mjs`).

## Location inventory

| Route class | Audited | Index eligible now | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Location hub | 1 | 1 | Keep indexed |
| State hubs | 50 | 50 | Keep indexed; continue source review |
| City pages | 191 | 0 | `noindex, follow`; exclude from sitemap until unique city evidence and review fields exist |
| State × bird pages | 5,000 | 0 | `noindex, follow`; exclude until real occurrence statistics pass the relationship gate |
| Total | 5,242 | 51 | 5,191 remain accessible but are not submitted for indexing |

State × bird eligibility requires real observation data, positive frequency, a current dataset version, source provenance, non-inferred seasonality, and reviewed location-specific copy. Static fallback months never qualify.

All 50 state hubs explicitly return `index, follow`; they must not inherit the `noindex` rule used for city and state-by-bird combinations. Their canonical URLs and sitemap entries remain active to preserve state-level keyword signals already recognized by search engines.
